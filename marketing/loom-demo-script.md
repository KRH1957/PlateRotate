**Prepared by:** Beacon, Marketing Agent — KRH Digital
**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Recording device:** Kevin's Pixel 8
**Target length:** 60 seconds (full version) / 15 seconds (hook version)
**Style:** Natural screen recording, light voiceover. Unpolished is fine.

## Before You Record

**Phone setup:**
- Brightness: all the way up (helps on recording)
- Notifications: Do Not Disturb on
- Font size: default or one size up for readability
- Orientation: portrait (vertical) — this is a mobile app demo
- Clear recent apps so the app opens fresh

**Loom setup:**
- Record front-facing camera OFF — screen only, plus your voice
- Or: silent screen recording with text overlays added in post (simpler option noted below)

## 60-Second Full Version

---

### BEAT 1 — Opening (0:00–0:05)

**Action:** Home screen visible, tap the PlateRotate app icon to open it.

**Screen state:** App splash / loading for 1–2 seconds, then the main screen appears.

**Say:**
> "This is PlateRotate. You pick your diet, type in any meal, and it tells you exactly how to eat it."

*5 seconds. No setup needed — jump straight into it.*

---

### BEAT 2 — Diet Selection (0:05–0:18)

**Action:** The diet selection screen is visible. Scroll slowly so the viewer can see all 6 options:
- Ketogenic
- Carnivore
- GLP-1 Friendly
- Mediterranean
- Paleo
- Anti-Inflammatory

Pause on **GLP-1 Friendly** for 2 full seconds. Then tap it.

**Say:**
> "Six diets — keto, carnivore, GLP-1 friendly for people on Ozempic or Wegovy, Mediterranean, paleo, anti-inflammatory."

Then, as you tap GLP-1 Friendly:
> "I'm going to pick GLP-1 friendly."

*13 seconds. The pause on GLP-1 Friendly is intentional — it's the most distinctive diet in the lineup and will catch attention from the 11 million people on these medications.*

---

### BEAT 3 — Meal Input (0:18–0:30)

**Action:** Navigate to the conversion screen. Tap the meal input field. Using the voice/mic button (or typing), enter:

> **"Chipotle burrito bowl with rice, black beans, chicken, sour cream, and cheese"**

*Type this in full — don't abbreviate. The specificity of the meal makes the demo more convincing.*

**Say:**
> "Now I type in what I want to eat. I'm going to use a Chipotle burrito bowl."

*[pause while typing or speaking the meal]*

> "Hit convert."

Tap the convert button.

*12 seconds. The moment of anticipation before the result lands is valuable — don't cut it out.*

---

### BEAT 4 — Conversion Result (0:30–0:50)

**Action:** The conversion result appears on screen. Scroll through it slowly so the viewer can read key parts. Pause on the most specific adaptation — for example, the guidance about rice (skip it on GLP-1) and what to do with the sour cream (reduce portion, it slows digestion).

**Say:**
> "Here's what it tells me. Skip the rice — too high glycemic, causes the blood sugar spike GLP-1 is working against. Keep the chicken and black beans. Go light on the sour cream — fat slows digestion and GLP-1 already does that."

*[scroll to show more of the result]*

> "This is the kind of answer you'd normally have to Google for 20 minutes — and still not get a clear yes or no."

*20 seconds. This is the money moment. Let the screen do the work. Don't rush it.*

---

### BEAT 5 — Close (0:50–1:00)

**Action:** Navigate back to the home screen or show the diet selection screen again.

**Say:**
> "No account. No sign-up. Five free conversions to try it. PlateRotate — at platerotate.com."

*10 seconds. End clean. Don't add "like and subscribe" or a long outro.*

---

## Notes for Recording

- **If you stumble on a word:** Keep going. This is a Loom demo, not a commercial. A natural pause or self-correction is fine — it reads as authentic.
- **If the API takes a moment to respond:** That pause is fine to leave in. It shows it's doing real work.
- **Speed:** Speak at a normal talking pace. Don't rush. People need a second to read what's on the screen while also listening to you.
- **One take is fine.** If you get through it reasonably clean, use it. You can always re-record once but don't wait for perfect.
