Hey Product Hunt — I'm Kevin, and I built PlateRotate.

Here's the problem I kept seeing: people on Ozempic, Wegovy, or a keto protocol sitting in a restaurant staring at a menu, or standing in someone's kitchen at a family dinner, or at a work lunch — not knowing what they can eat. Not needing a long explanation. Just needing a fast, clear answer.

"Can I eat this? If not, how do I adapt it?"

That's what PlateRotate does. You pick your diet, type in any meal — "Chipotle burrito bowl," "my mother-in-law's lasagna," "Olive Garden chicken alfredo" — and it tells you exactly how to eat it within your protocol. What to keep, what to swap, what to skip. No macros, no calorie counting, no five-step process.

**Six diets, all included from day one:**
- Ketogenic
- Carnivore
- GLP-1 Friendly (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound)
- Mediterranean
- Paleo
- Anti-Inflammatory

**On the privacy side:** There's no account. No sign-up. No food diary stored on a server. No profile built on you over time. You type a meal, you get an answer, nothing about you is collected or tracked. For people managing their health with medication or navigating sensitive dietary needs, that matters.

**Pricing:**
- Free: 5 conversions — enough to see if it works for you
- Basic: $2.99/month or $29/year
- Pro: $5.99/month or $59/year — includes allergen filters and the full feature set

Right now it's live on Android (Play Store + direct APK at platerotate.com). iOS is coming.

I'm a one-person team. This app exists because I think there's a real gap between the $15/month diet apps that do everything and the nothing-at-all experience people have when they're actually standing in front of a meal they didn't choose.

If you try it — what meal did you use? What diet are you on? I'd genuinely like to know where it works well and where it needs to be sharper.

Thanks for checking it out.

## Optimal Launch Day and Time

**Recommended: Tuesday or Wednesday, live by 12:01 AM Pacific time.**

**Why Tuesday or Wednesday:**
Product Hunt resets at midnight Pacific every day. The first 6–8 hours of traffic determine whether you rank in the daily top 5. Tuesdays and Wednesdays consistently have the highest active voter counts — the PH audience is heaviest mid-week. Mondays are competitive but traffic is softer (people re-entering the work week). Thursday drops off. Friday is the worst day — community attention is fragmented.

**Why 12:01 AM Pacific:**
Products submitted right at midnight get the full 24-hour window. Launching at 9 AM or noon means you've already burned 9–12 hours of your voting window. Submit at 12:01 AM Pacific and the entire PH community in every time zone gets to discover you throughout their day.

**Pre-launch prep (the day before):**
- Tell your existing users, email list, and every community you've been active in: "We're launching on Product Hunt tomorrow at midnight Pacific — would love your support."
- Post in r/ProductHunt if you have account history there.
- Schedule social posts to go live the moment the PH listing is up.

**Avoid:**
- Product launches by major companies — they dominate the day and suppress smaller listings
- US holidays and the week between Christmas and New Year
